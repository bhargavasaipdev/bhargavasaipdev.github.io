Privacy Policy for ReportCraft

Application: ReportCraft (com.aisyntax.reportcraft)  |  Developer: Ai Syntax – Bhargava Sai Pinninti

ReportCraft (“we,” “our,” or “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application, ReportCraft (the “App”), handles information when you use our services.

1

100% Offline Local Data Storage & Processing

ReportCraft is designed to operate primarily offline to ensure maximum data privacy for educators, teachers, school administrators, and academic institutions:

  • Local Student Records: All student names, roll numbers, raw marks, subject data, and academic results are processed and stored strictly on your local device storage.
  • Local School Branding Assets: All institution names, addresses, logos, banners, class teacher signatures, and principal signatures remain exclusively on your local device.
  • Local Document Exports: All generated progress reports, PDFs, and PNG images are saved locally to your device storage (Documents/ReportCraft).
  • Zero Cloud Uploads: We do not operate external cloud servers or databases to collect, store, or transmit your student records or academic data.
2

Information Collected by Third-Party Advertising Services (Google AdMob)

To support the ongoing development and maintenance of ReportCraft, our App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements (Banner Ads, Interstitial Ads, Rewarded Video Ads, and Native Ads).

Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain non-personally identifiable information from your device, including:

  • Unique Device Identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID / AAID)
  • IP Address and approximate location
  • App interaction data and ad performance statistics
  • System diagnostic and crash logs

This information is collected directly by Google AdMob for ad serving, analytics, personalization, and fraud prevention purposes.

For more information on how Google handles advertising data, please review Google’s Privacy Policy.

3

App Permissions & Usage

ReportCraft requests the following permissions to perform its core offline functionalities and ad operations:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required by Google AdMob to fetch advertisement content.
  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE & WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (API 26-32): Required on legacy Android devices to read imported spreadsheets and save generated PDF/PNG report cards.
  • Storage Access Framework / File Provider (API 33+): Uses Android’s native DocumentFile and FileProvider APIs to allow secure file saving, opening, and sharing without requiring broad storage permissions.
4

Children’s Privacy

ReportCraft is an administrative tool intended for use by teachers, educators, school administrators, and institutions. The App does not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from children under the age of 13. All student data entered by educators is stored locally on the user’s device and is not collected or transmitted by us.

5

Data Security

Because all your student records and school branding assets are stored locally on your device, the security of your data depends on your device’s security settings (such as screen locks and device encryption). We recommend keeping your device passcode-protected.

6

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be effective immediately upon posting the updated Privacy Policy on this page.
