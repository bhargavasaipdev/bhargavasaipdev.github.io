DocsAlert Privacy Policy

Developer: Ai Syntax – Bhargava Sai Pinninti

Welcome to DocsAlert, operated by Ai Syntax (“we”, “us”, or “our”). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data and document information you manage using our application.

This Privacy Policy explains how DocsAlert handles your information, what data is processed, and your rights regarding your data.

1

Information Collection and Storage

A. Local On-Device Data (100% Private to You)

DocsAlert is designed with a privacy-first, on-device storage architecture:

  • Document & Credential Metadata: All document names, categories, issue/expiry dates, notes, and credential numbers (PAN, Aadhaar, Passport, etc.) are saved locally on your device in a private, isolated SQLite database.
  • Item Vault & Attachment Files: All photos and PDF files stored in the Item Vault or attached to Family Member profiles are saved in DocsAlert’s private internal storage directory on your phone.
  • No Server Uploads: DocsAlert does not operate external cloud servers or databases. Your document metadata, photos, and files are never uploaded, transmitted, or shared with Ai Syntax or any external third party.

B. App Preferences & Security Toggles

Your preferences (Theme settings, Notification sound choices, Daily alarm time, App Lock settings, and Number masking toggles) are stored locally on your device via Android DataStore.

2

Third-Party Services and Advertising (Google AdMob)

DocsAlert uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to serve advertisements within the application. While DocsAlert does not collect or transmit your personal documents, Google AdMob may automatically process certain technical device identifiers to deliver relevant ads, prevent ad fraud, and provide analytics:

Information Processed by Google AdMob:

  • Device Advertising ID (GAID)
  • IP Address & General Location (Country/City level)
  • App Interaction & Performance Diagnostics
  • Crash logs and System Performance metrics

This data is processed directly by Google in accordance with Google’s Privacy Policy.

3

Device Permissions Requested

DocsAlert requests specific Android permissions strictly to provide its core features:

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Required to send you timely expiry alerts and daily task notifications.
  • Exact Alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM / USE_EXACT_ALARM): Required to trigger alarm alerts precisely at your scheduled time.
  • Full-Screen Alerts (USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT): Allows full-screen caller-style alarm overlays over the lock screen for critical document expiration alerts.
  • Biometrics (USE_BIOMETRIC / USE_FINGERPRINT): Used locally on-device to verify your identity when unlocking the optional App Lock feature.
4

Data Security and User Control

  • On-Device Security: You can enable Biometric App Lock in Settings to protect DocsAlert when backgrounded.
  • Masking Sensitive Numbers: You can enable Mask Sensitive Numbers in Settings to obscure document numbers by default.
  • Backup & Restore: DocsAlert allows you to export your data and files to a local JSON backup file. You retain full control over where this file is stored and shared.
  • Data Deletion: Since all data is stored locally, uninstalling DocsAlert or clearing the app data in Android Settings permanently deletes all stored items, family profiles, and vault files from your device.
5

Children’s Privacy

DocsAlert is a general-purpose document management and reminder application intended for adult users. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
