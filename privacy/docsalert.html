Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 1, 2024 | Developer: Ai Syntax

1. Information Collection and Local Storage

DocsAlert operates with a strict privacy-first, on-device storage architecture:

  • Local Document Metadata: Document names, categories, issue/expiry dates, notes, and numbers (PAN, Aadhaar, Passport) are saved 100% locally on your phone using an encrypted SQLite database.
  • Item Vault Attachments: All photo files and PDFs stored in the Item Vault remain safely stored in your device's private internal directory.
  • No Server Uploads: DocsAlert does not run cloud servers. Your documents and personal files are never uploaded or shared with the developer or any external server.

2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

DocsAlert uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to serve advertisements. AdMob may process certain technical device identifiers to deliver relevant ads and prevent fraud:

  • Device Advertising ID (GAID)
  • IP Address & General Location
  • App Interaction Logs & Crash Diagnostics

3. Requested Android Permissions

  • Notifications: Needed to send timely expiry alerts and daily task notifications.
  • Exact Alarms: Needed to trigger scheduled alarm reminders accurately.
  • Biometrics: Used locally to verify identity when opening the optional App Lock.
